# Refboard

A reference board for interior design students. Keep every reference for a design project in one place: photos, Pinterest links, files, sizes, materials and prices.

**Try it:** https://izza521.github.io/refs-test/

## What it does

- **Projects:** one project per design brief
- **Refs by category:** mood & inspiration, furniture, materials & finishes, lighting, plans & drawings, site & rooms, lecturer feedback
- **Measurements:** W × D × H in mm, cm, m, in or ft, with automatic conversion and floor area for rooms
- **Files:** attach PDFs, Word files, DWG, SketchUp and more
- **Search and filters:** by material, room, style, supplier or file name
- **Furniture & finishes schedule:** a priced FF&E table you can copy or download for Excel
- **Colour palettes:** pulled from each photo and from the whole project
- **Tidy score and badges:** a nudge to fill in the details
- **Mochi:** a little cat who keeps you company while you work

## How it works

Everything is in one `index.html` file (HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no frameworks). Data is saved in the browser with IndexedDB, so nothing is uploaded and it works without an account. You can back up and restore your data from the ⋯ menu.
